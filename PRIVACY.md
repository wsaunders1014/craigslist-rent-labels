# Rent Labels privacy policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Rent Labels is a browser extension that labels rental map pins with rent and bedrooms and flags listings that look like single rooms or coliving on Craigslist, apartments.com and Zillow.

## What Rent Labels collects

Nothing. Rent Labels has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or server of its own. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or browsing history.

## What it reads, and where that stays

On Craigslist housing searches, apartments.com and zillow.com, Rent Labels reads listing information those pages have already loaded (such as price, bedrooms, titles and map positions) so it can label map pins and result cards. This happens entirely in your browser. Rent Labels does not send this information anywhere, and it does not make its own requests to these sites. Craigslist listing previews may load thumbnail images from Craigslist, as Craigslist's own page does.

## What it stores

Rent Labels saves your display preferences (how grouped markers behave, how many rows to show, and how to treat possible rooms) with Chrome's extension storage on your device. You can clear them by removing the extension.

## Donations

The welcome and settings pages include a Donate button. It opens PayPal in a new tab only when you click it. Anything you do on PayPal is covered by PayPal's privacy policy, not this one; Rent Labels receives no information about who donates.
