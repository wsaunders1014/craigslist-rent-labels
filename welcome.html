Rent Labels is installed

Rent and bedrooms right on rental maps, and a heads-up when a “unit” is really a single room or coliving.

What it does

  • Craigslist: map markers show price and bedrooms, repeated ads are grouped, and grouped markers open a list instead of zooming.
  • apartments.com and Zillow: map pins show price and bedrooms. Result cards and pins that look like a room or coliving are tinted yellow; hover a card to see it normally.

Room detection reads wording and sizes, so it will sometimes be wrong.

Changing or turning it off

  • Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome’s toolbar and pin Rent Labels. Its button opens settings, where you can fade or hide possible rooms, or switch flagging off.
  • To turn the whole extension off, right-click its button, choose Manage extension, and switch it off. Switch it back on any time.

Privacy

No accounts, tracking, or analytics. Rent Labels only reads what these sites already show you, and nothing leaves your browser.
